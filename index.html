Herm

A personal desktop assistant for Gmail and Google Calendar.

Herm is a private, single-user assistant that runs locally on its owner's Windows PC. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

When the owner signs in with Google and grants access, Herm can:

  • Read, search, summarize, and draft or send Gmail messages on the owner's request
  • View, create, and update Google Calendar events on the owner's request

The owner interacts with Herm through a private Discord bot. All processing happens on the owner's own computer using a locally hosted language model.

Privacy

Herm does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
